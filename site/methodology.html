Methodology

How this dataset is built

The Indian Job Market Explorer combines official classification and survey sources with clearly labeled proxy fields. It is a research-grade visualization, not an official forecast or census.

Source stack

NCO 2015

Used for occupation codes, hierarchy, titles, and skill levels. This project aggregates to 43 two-digit occupation groups.

PLFS 2023-24

Used for employment distribution (Table 25) and earnings (Table 50) from the Annual Report, July 2023 – June 2024. Employment counts are derived from the official percentage distribution × total workforce (WPR 43.7% × 1.428B = 625M). Earnings are for regular wage/salaried employees at the NCO Division level.

NCS

Used for demand signals and occupation descriptions where available. Vacancy counts are a portal-derived proxy, not total labour demand.

NSDC

Used to enrich education and training context with qualification-pack style competency metadata.

Field notes

  • Employment is computed from the PLFS Table 25 percentage distribution (usual status ps+ss, rural+urban, persons) applied to a total workforce of ~625 million (WPR 43.7% × projected population 1.428B). Armed forces figures (~1.4M) are from Ministry of Defence data, as PLFS covers civilian population only.
  • Pay is from PLFS Table 50: average monthly wage/salary earnings for regular wage/salaried employees in CWS, reported at the NCO 1-digit Division level. This covers only ~21.7% of workers (salaried). For context, PLFS Statement 12 reports self-employment gross earnings of ~₹13,900 over the last 30 days in April-June 2024, and Statement 11 reports casual labour earnings of ~₹433/day in April-June 2024.
  • Demand Index comes from NCS vacancy signals and must be read as a directional proxy.
  • AI Exposure is an LLM-scored estimate calibrated for the Indian labour market, not an official statistic.
Important: India does not publish a BLS-style occupation forecast. This project does not claim to predict future employment levels.

Current production limits

  • The published frontend is fully deployable as a static site.
  • The repo includes committed build artifacts, CI validation, and GitHub Pages deployment.
  • Some descriptive fields still fall back to taxonomy text when live NCS scraping data is unavailable.
